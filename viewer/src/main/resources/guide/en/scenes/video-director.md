The video director makes films of the world. You fly the free camera, and Cruma turns a few views into a smooth flight.
It works on every client, also on one with no cut scenes, such as Interlude.

![The parts of the director: the path in the world, the tray under the world, and the panel at the right.](diagram:director-parts)

## Keys and the path

A [key](guide:terms/key) keeps one view of the camera at one moment: its place, which way it looks, its roll and its
field of view. Between the keys Cruma draws a curve, the [camera path](guide:terms/camera-path), and the camera flies
along it. Two keys make a flight. More keys make it bend and slow down where you want.

A film can have more than one [shot](guide:terms/shot). Where a new shot starts, the camera jumps to another place, as
in a film made of cuts.

## The tray

**New** under **MY SCENES** opens a scene in the tray under the world. The tray has the **Add** menu, **Camera view**,
**Set key**, **New shot** and **Delete**, and under them a ruler with the [playhead](guide:terms/playhead), the moment
you work on. Each key stands on the **Camera** track. Once a scene has more, it gets tracks for the bars, the screen,
the music, the voice lines, the sounds and each actor.

## The world and the panel

In the world the path shows as a line, and each key as a small camera that looks the way the key looks. The panel at
the right shows the scene and what you chose, such as a key with **Take the view**, **Look through** and **Ease**.

## More than a flight

From the **Add** menu a scene also gets NPCs, characters and an airship that walk and cast skills, music and voice
lines, fades, lines of text and slides. The copy button that shows on a row of a cut scene under the pointer opens that
scene in the tray, so you can change a scene of the client.

## A scene is a file

**Save** writes the scene as a [scene file](guide:terms/scene-file) of your own. A scene can leave as an MP4 video, as
a folder for Blender, or go into a client in place of one of its cut scenes.
