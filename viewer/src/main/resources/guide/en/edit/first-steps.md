Open a place of your client in Edit, learn where things are, and go back to the Viewer.

### You need

- A Lineage II client on this computer. See [Open a world](guide:start/open-a-world).

## Open Edit

1. Choose the client and the tiles on the start screen as for the Viewer, choose **Edit** beside **View** in the bar
   under the map, and press **Open**. Or, in the Viewer, press **Edit** on the top bar.
2. Wait while the tiles load. From the Viewer, a card over the status bar counts them, such as "Loading tiles for
   editing · 3 of 40", and the tiles nearest to the camera come first.
3. Read the card in the middle of the view, **Edit this map**. **All keys (F1)** lists every key, and **Got it** closes
   the card for good.

[Open the Select tool](action:edit-tool-select)

## Find your way

- The top bar says Edit and the name of the client. The menu at its right end has **Back to Viewer** and **Main
  menu**.
- The Edit panel on the right shows what is selected and what is not saved, with Undo and Redo as icons. Under it
  are the tools as tabs: **Select**, **Place**, **Sculpt**, **Paint**, **Lights** and **Surfaces**, with their keys
  <kbd>Q</kbd>, <kbd>P</kbd>, <kbd>T</kbd>, <kbd>B</kbd>, <kbd>I</kbd> and <kbd>U</kbd>. At the bottom, **Save…** opens
  the save review. Drag the panel's left edge to make it wider.
- The Scene panel on the left has two tabs. **Objects** lists what the loaded maps hold, by mesh. The chips
  **All**, **Selected** and **Changed** filter it. **Library** holds the meshes you can place.
- The hint strip at the top of the view shows the keys of the open tool.

Hold the right mouse button and use <kbd>W</kbd>, <kbd>A</kbd>, <kbd>S</kbd> and <kbd>D</kbd> to fly, as in the
Viewer. <kbd>F2</kbd> does not switch to Edit or out of it, as that loads the tiles again.

## Leave Edit

1. Open the menu at the right end of the top bar, and choose **Back to Viewer** or **Main menu**.
2. If changes are not saved, the dialog **Unsaved changes** asks first. **Save…** stays and opens the save review.
   **Leave and keep the draft** leaves, and Edit offers the changes back next time. **Discard** drops them.

When it worked, the Viewer opens over the same place, and the tiles load again.

### If it went wrong

- An object cannot be clicked: its tile may still be loading. Wait until the card over the status bar is gone.
- The dialog **Recover the Edit session** opens as Edit starts: an earlier session stopped before it saved. **Recover**
  brings the changes back, **Discard** drops them, and **Back to Viewer** keeps them for later.
- The panel is gone: press <kbd>Tab</kbd>, or the box icon on the rail at the right.
