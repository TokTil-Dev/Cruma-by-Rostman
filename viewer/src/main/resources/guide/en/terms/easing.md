---
title: Easing
kind: term
summary: How the camera passes a key: on a smooth curve that keeps its speed, or at a corner of straight lines.
related: scenes/keys-and-shots
---
**Ease** in the panel of a chosen key has **Smooth** and **Corner**. Smooth bends the path through the key. Corner makes
the path come and go on straight lines, and the turn and the field of view move evenly there. See [Keys, shots and
easing](guide:scenes/keys-and-shots).
