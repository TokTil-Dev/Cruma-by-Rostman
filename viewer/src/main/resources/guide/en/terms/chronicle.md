---
title: Chronicle
kind: term
summary: A version of Lineage II, such as Interlude or High Five, each with maps and files of its own.
related: start/clients-and-tiles
---
Cruma does not ask which chronicle a client is: it reads what the files hold. It was tested with C1, Interlude, Gracia
Final, High Five, P152 and P162. Some newer clients also hold a Classic world beside the main one, and Essence clients a third, Essence. See [Clients,
chronicles and tiles](guide:start/clients-and-tiles).
