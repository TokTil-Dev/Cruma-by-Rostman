---
title: Mover
kind: term
summary: An actor that moves in the game by itself, such as a door, a gate or a lift.
related: edit/what-edit-changes, terms/actor
---
Its name in a map is often like `Mover0`. Edit cannot change a mover: a click on one says "Doors and other movers
cannot be edited." See [What Edit can change](guide:edit/what-edit-changes).
