---
title: Particle
kind: term
summary: One small picture or mesh that an emitter throws: it is born, moves, fades and dies in a short time.
related: effects/what-are-effects
---
A spark, a drop of rain or a puff of smoke is one particle. An [emitter](guide:terms/emitter) throws many of them, each
with its own place, speed, colour and life. The card of an effect in the **Effects** panel counts the particles alive
while it plays. See [What are effects](guide:effects/what-are-effects).
