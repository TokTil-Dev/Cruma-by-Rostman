---
title: Lightmap
kind: term
summary: The light of a map baked into pictures over its surfaces, so the game does not work it out as you play.
related: view/scene-settings
---
The [BSP](guide:terms/bsp) of a map has lightmaps. They show in the view Lit, and not in Unlit. See [Change the light,
sky, water and sound](guide:view/scene-settings).
