---
title: Region
kind: term
summary: One geodata file: the cells of one tile of the map, 32768 units on each side.
related: geodata/cells-layers-nswe
---
A region has the name of the [tile](guide:terms/tile) it covers, such as `22_22`, and holds 256 by 256
[blocks](guide:terms/block). Its file is `22_22_conv.dat` in [L2OFF](guide:terms/l2off) or `22_22.l2j` in
[L2J](guide:terms/l2j). See [Cells, layers and NSWE](guide:geodata/cells-layers-nswe).
