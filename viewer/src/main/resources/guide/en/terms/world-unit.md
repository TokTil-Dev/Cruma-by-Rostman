---
title: World unit
kind: term
summary: The measure of length in Lineage II, in which X points east, Y south and Z up.
related: geodata/cells-layers-nswe
---
A [cell](guide:terms/cell) is 16 units wide and a [tile](guide:terms/tile) 32768. The game, the server's files and
Cruma give places in the same units, so a point such as `82100, 147500, -3400` means the same place everywhere. See
[Cells, layers and NSWE](guide:geodata/cells-layers-nswe).
