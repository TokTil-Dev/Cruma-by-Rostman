---
title: Respawn
kind: term
summary: How long the server waits after an NPC dies before it puts a new one in its place.
related: spawns/what-is-a-spawn, spawns/edit-a-spawn
---
A respawn time is a number with `sec`, `min`, `hour` or `day`, such as `30sec`, or `no` for an NPC that never comes
back. A random part, `respawn_rand`, makes it a little shorter or longer each time. See
[What is a spawn](guide:spawns/what-is-a-spawn).
