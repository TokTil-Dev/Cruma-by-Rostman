---
title: Actor
kind: term
summary: Anything placed in a map with a place, a turn and a size: a mesh, a light, a sound or an effect.
related: start/how-a-map-is-built
---
A map is a list of actors over its [terrain](guide:terms/terrain) and [BSP](guide:terms/bsp). The Object panel names the
actor of what you click, and Edit moves actors. See [How a map is built](guide:start/how-a-map-is-built).
