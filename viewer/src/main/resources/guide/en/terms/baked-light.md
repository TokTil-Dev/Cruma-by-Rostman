---
title: Baked light
kind: term
summary: Light worked out once, when the map is built, and saved with it, so the game does not work it out as you play.
related: edit/lights, terms/lightmap
---
Walls and floors keep it in [lightmaps](guide:terms/lightmap), meshes and the ground keep a colour at each of their
points. So when a light or an object moves, the saved light is out of date until it is built again. See [Lights and
why light is built again](guide:edit/lights).
