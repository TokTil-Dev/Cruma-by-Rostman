---
title: Texture
kind: term
summary: A picture painted on a surface, such as the bark of a tree or the stones of a wall.
related: view/inspect-an-object
---
Textures are kept in `.utx` packages in `Textures`. A [material](guide:terms/material) names the textures a surface uses
and how they mix. See [Inspect an object and its materials](guide:view/inspect-an-object).
