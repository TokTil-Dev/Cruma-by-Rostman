---
title: Cell
kind: term
summary: The smallest square of geodata, 16 by 16 world units, with one floor or more.
related: geodata/cells-layers-nswe
---
The server checks each step of a character from cell to cell. A cell keeps a height and four sides for each of its
[layers](guide:terms/layer). See [Cells, layers and NSWE](guide:geodata/cells-layers-nswe).
