---
title: Gizmo
kind: term
summary: The arrows, rings and handles on a selected object that you drag to move, turn or size it.
related: edit/select-and-move, terms/pivot, terms/snapping
---
Edit has three: Move with arrows, Rotate with rings and Scale with small boxes. <kbd>W</kbd>, <kbd>E</kbd> and
<kbd>R</kbd> choose one. The gizmo stands on the [pivot](guide:terms/pivot) of the selection. See [Select and
move objects](guide:edit/select-and-move).
