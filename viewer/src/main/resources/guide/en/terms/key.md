---
title: Key
kind: term
summary: One view of the camera kept at one moment of a scene: its place, its turn, its roll and its field of view.
related: scenes/keys-and-shots, terms/camera-path
---
<kbd>K</kbd> or **Set key** in the tray of the video director puts a key at the playhead with what the camera shows. The
camera flies from key to key along the [camera path](guide:terms/camera-path). An order to an actor, a fade or a sound
is also a key on its own track. See [Keys, shots and easing](guide:scenes/keys-and-shots).
