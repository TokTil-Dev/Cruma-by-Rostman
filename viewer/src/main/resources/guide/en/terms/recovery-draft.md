---
title: Recovery draft
kind: term
summary: A copy of your unsaved Edit changes that Cruma keeps as you work, so a crash does not lose them.
related: edit/save-and-check, edit/first-steps
---
The next time you open Edit, the dialog **Recover the Edit session** offers it back with **Recover**. Once a save takes
all the changes, the draft goes away. It does not keep painted layers, layer mappings or changed cells of the ground
yet. See [Save, install and check in the game](guide:edit/save-and-check).
