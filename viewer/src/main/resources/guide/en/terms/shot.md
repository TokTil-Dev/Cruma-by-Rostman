---
title: Shot
kind: term
summary: One stretch of a film in which the camera moves without a jump, until a cut starts the next shot.
related: scenes/keys-and-shots, scenes/what-is-a-cut-scene
---
In a cut scene of the client, a shot looks at an actor or a point from a distance and an angle. In the video director, a
shot is a part of the camera path between two cuts, and the **Camera** track draws a span for it, such as `Shot 2`. See
[Keys, shots and easing](guide:scenes/keys-and-shots).
