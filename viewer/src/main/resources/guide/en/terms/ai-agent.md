---
title: AI agent
kind: term
summary: A program with an AI model, such as Claude Code, that does work you ask for in words by using tools.
related: agent/what-agents-do
---
With Cruma connected, an agent can look at the world, read, edit, film and export, within the [access
level](guide:terms/access-level) you set. The **AI agent** panel shows each call it makes. See [What an AI agent can
do](guide:agent/what-agents-do).
