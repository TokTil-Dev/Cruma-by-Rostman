---
title: Camera path
kind: term
summary: The curve the camera of the video director flies along, through its keys in time.
related: scenes/video-director, terms/key
---
Cruma draws the curve between the [keys](guide:terms/key), so the speed does not jump at a key, and a turn goes the
short way. The world shows the path as a line, solid in the open and dashed behind walls. See [The video
director](guide:scenes/video-director).
