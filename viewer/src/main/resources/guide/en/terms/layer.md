---
title: Layer
kind: term
summary: One floor of a cell: its height, and which of its four sides are open.
related: geodata/cells-layers-nswe, geodata/edit-cells
---
A cell under a bridge has two layers, one on the bridge and one on the ground. The server takes the layer at the feet
of a character. In the Inspect tool of the **Geodata** panel, **FLOORS IN THIS CELL** lists them. See
[Cells, layers and NSWE](guide:geodata/cells-layers-nswe). To give a cell a new layer, use the **Floor** tool or
**Add layer above**, as [Edit cells](guide:geodata/edit-cells) says.
