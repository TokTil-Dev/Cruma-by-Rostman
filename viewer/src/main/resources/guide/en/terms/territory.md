---
title: Territory
kind: term
summary: A zone of npcpos.txt: a shape of three or more corners, with a bottom and a top height.
related: spawns/what-is-a-spawn, terms/maker
---
A territory is where a [maker](guide:terms/maker) puts its random NPCs: on ground inside the shape, between its bottom
and its top. The **NPC Spawns** panel draws it as a coloured outline on the ground. A maker can also leave out the
part of a zone that another territory covers. See [What is a spawn](guide:spawns/what-is-a-spawn).
