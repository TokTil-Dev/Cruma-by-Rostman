---
title: Material
kind: term
summary: What a surface is made of: its textures, how they mix, and whether light passes through it.
related: view/inspect-an-object
---
A mesh has one material for each slot, so a tree can have bark and leaves. The Material tab of the Object panel shows
each slot with its material and [textures](guide:terms/texture). See [Inspect an object and its
materials](guide:view/inspect-an-object).
