---
title: BSP
kind: term
summary: The built geometry of a map, such as walls, floors and the insides of buildings, cut from solid shapes.
related: start/how-a-map-is-built
---
BSP stands for binary space partitioning, the way the engine sorts these surfaces. Its light is baked into
[lightmaps](guide:terms/lightmap). Scene settings calls it CSG Geometry. See [How a map is
built](guide:start/how-a-map-is-built).
