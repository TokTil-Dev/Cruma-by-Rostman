---
title: Material slot
kind: term
summary: One part of a mesh that wears its own material, such as the bark or the leaves of a tree.
related: edit/surfaces-and-materials, terms/material
---
The mesh gives each slot a material. An object can wear another [material](guide:terms/material) in a slot without
changing the mesh, so other objects of the same mesh stay as they are. See [Surfaces, materials and
textures](guide:edit/surfaces-and-materials).
