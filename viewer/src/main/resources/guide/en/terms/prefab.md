---
title: Prefab
kind: term
summary: A group of objects saved to a file, so you can place the whole group again in one click.
related: edit/place-from-the-library
---
A prefab keeps each object's mesh, its place in the group, its turn, its size and its materials. Edit saves one with
**Save as prefab…** and lists the prefabs of that folder in the Library. See [Place objects from the
Library](guide:edit/place-from-the-library).
