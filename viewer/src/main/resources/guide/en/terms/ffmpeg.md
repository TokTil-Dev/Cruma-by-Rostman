---
title: ffmpeg
kind: term
summary: A free program that makes video files, which Cruma needs for an MP4 export.
related: scenes/export-a-scene
---
Cruma sends it the frames and the sound of a scene, and it writes an MP4 file with H.264 and AAC. Without it **Export
MP4** is off, and its tip says how to install it on your system. See [Export a scene as a video or to
Blender](guide:scenes/export-a-scene).
