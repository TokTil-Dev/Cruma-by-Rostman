---
title: Maker
kind: term
summary: The part of npcpos.txt that fills one or more zones with NPCs: which ones, how many, and how often.
related: spawns/what-is-a-spawn, terms/territory, terms/respawn
---
A maker lists entries. Each entry names an NPC, a count, a [respawn](guide:terms/respawn) time, and whether the NPC
stands anywhere in the zone or at a fixed point. Its `maximum_npc` is how many of its NPCs the server keeps alive at
once. See [What is a spawn](guide:spawns/what-is-a-spawn).
