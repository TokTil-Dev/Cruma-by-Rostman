---
title: Pivot
kind: term
summary: The point an object turns and grows around, and the point its Location names.
related: edit/select-and-move, terms/gizmo
---
The pivot of a mesh is set when the mesh is made, often at its foot or its middle. For several objects, **Pivot** in
the Edit panel chooses **Center**, **Active** or **Each**. See [Select and move objects](guide:edit/select-and-move).
