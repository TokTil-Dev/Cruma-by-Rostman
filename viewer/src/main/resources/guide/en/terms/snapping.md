---
title: Snapping
kind: term
summary: Moving in fixed steps, so objects line up: a step of distance, of angle or of size.
related: edit/select-and-move, terms/gizmo
---
In Edit, **Grid** snaps a move to 16 units at first, **Angle** a turn to 15 degrees and **Scale** a size to steps of
0.1. Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>, or <kbd>Cmd</kbd> on a Mac, during a drag to turn it the other way. See [Select and move
objects](guide:edit/select-and-move).
