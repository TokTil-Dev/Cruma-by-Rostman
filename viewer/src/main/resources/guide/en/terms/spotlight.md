---
title: Spotlight
kind: term
summary: A light that shines one way in a cone, like a torch, where a plain light shines all around.
related: edit/lights
---
A spotlight has a direction, given as a yaw and a pitch, and a **Cone** that says how wide it opens. In the Lights tool
its card has a compass and a side view to turn it. See [Lights and why light is built again](guide:edit/lights).
