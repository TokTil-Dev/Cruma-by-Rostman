---
title: npcdata.txt
kind: term
summary: The server's file of NPCs: for each id its name, level, stats, skills, AI and drops.
related: npcs/what-is-npc-data, npcs/npc-files
---
`npcdata.txt` lies in the server's [script folder](guide:terms/script-folder), one NPC a line. The server counts every
fight with its numbers, and players never see it. The **NPCs** panel edits it, and **NPC Spawns** reads its levels and
sizes. See [What is NPC data](guide:npcs/what-is-npc-data).
