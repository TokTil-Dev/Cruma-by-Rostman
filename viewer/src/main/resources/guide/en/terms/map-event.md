---
title: Map event
kind: term
summary: A number on effects of a map: the client hides them until the server sends that number.
related: events/what-are-map-events, events/switch-events
---
Many event numbers start with their tile, such as `22226292` in `22_22`. The client has no names for them, and an
L2OFF server names many of them in its `areadata.txt`. The **Map events** panel turns each one on or off in the view
and in exports. See [What are map events](guide:events/what-are-map-events).
