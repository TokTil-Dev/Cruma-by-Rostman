---
title: Game day
kind: term
summary: The clock of the game world: a day of 24 game hours that lasts 4 real hours.
related: events/effects-by-time
---
Some effects of a map show only in some hours of the game day, such as rain from 02:00 to 02:10. A window of 10
minutes passes in 1 minute and 40 seconds of real time. In Cruma, **Time of day** under Atmosphere in Scene settings
sets the hour. See [Effects by time of day](guide:events/effects-by-time).
