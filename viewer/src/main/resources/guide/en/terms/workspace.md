---
title: Workspace
kind: term
summary: How much of the interface the Viewer shows: Explore, Inspect, Analyze or Edit.
related: start/moving-around
---
Explore shows the world alone. Inspect selects what you click and shows it in the Object panel. Analyze adds the list of
objects and a tray at the bottom, and Edit lets you change the map. See [Move around the
world](guide:start/moving-around).
