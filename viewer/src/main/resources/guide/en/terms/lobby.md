---
title: Lobby
kind: term
summary: The map behind the login and character screens of a client, such as Lobby01, with camera scenes of its own.
related: scenes/into-a-client
---
A lobby is not a part of the world, so it has no tiles. Its camera sees as on the login screen: the field of view, 50
degrees in every client as it is installed, is the width of the picture, so a wide window shows less above and below
than in the world. Its camera scenes play from the **Scenes** panel, and the video director can copy and change them,
see [Put a scene into a client or a lobby](guide:scenes/into-a-client).

Edit changes a lobby as it changes a tile: its objects, its lights and its ground. See
[Sculpt the ground](guide:edit/sculpt-the-ground).
