---
title: glTF
kind: term
summary: An open file format for 3D scenes that Blender, Unity, Unreal and many other programs read.
related: export/export-a-location
---
Export location writes one `.glb` file, the form of glTF that holds the meshes, materials and textures in one file. See
[Export a location to Blender](guide:export/export-a-location).
