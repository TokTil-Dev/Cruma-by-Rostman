---
title: npcpos.txt
kind: term
summary: The file of an L2OFF server that says where its NPCs spawn, how many and how often.
related: spawns/what-is-a-spawn, spawns/spawn-formats
---
`npcpos.txt` lies in the server's [script folder](guide:terms/script-folder). It draws zones on the map, called
[territories](guide:terms/territory), and lists [makers](guide:terms/maker) that fill them with NPCs. The **NPC Spawns**
panel reads and edits it. See [What is a spawn](guide:spawns/what-is-a-spawn).
