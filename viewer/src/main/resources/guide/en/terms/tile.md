---
title: Tile
kind: term
summary: A square of the map 32768 units wide, named by its column and row, such as 22_22.
related: geodata/cells-layers-nswe
---
The maps of the client and the regions of geodata use the same grid of tiles. Tile `20_18` starts at X 0 and Y 0, and
each step in a name is one tile further east or south. The top bar of the Viewer names the tile under the camera. In
geodata a tile is a [region](guide:terms/region).
