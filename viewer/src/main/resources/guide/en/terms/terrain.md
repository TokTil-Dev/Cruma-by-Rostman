---
title: Terrain
kind: term
summary: The open ground of a tile: a grid of heights painted with several textures, such as grass and rock.
related: start/how-a-map-is-built
---
Each of its layers is a texture with an alpha map that says where the texture shows. Holes in it let caves and buildings
go under the ground. See [How a map is built](guide:start/how-a-map-is-built).
