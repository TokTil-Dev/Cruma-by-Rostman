---
title: Client
kind: term
summary: The folder of the game on your computer, with the maps, textures, meshes and sounds of its world.
related: start/clients-and-tiles
---
Cruma opens a client to draw its world, and needs the folders `Maps`, `Textures`, `StaticMeshes` and `system` in it. It
reads the client and changes nothing there unless you choose to: install a save, effects or a scene, build geodata, or copy tiles. See [Clients, chronicles
and tiles](guide:start/clients-and-tiles).
