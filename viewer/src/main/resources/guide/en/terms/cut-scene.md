---
title: Cut scene
kind: term
summary: A short film the game plays in the world, such as before a boss fight, with shots, actors and sound.
related: scenes/what-is-a-cut-scene, scenes/play-a-cut-scene
---
The server starts a cut scene with a number, and the client plays it around the player. The client keeps its cut scenes
in `System/LineageSceneInfo.u`, from Gracia Final on. The **Scenes** panel plays them in the world. See [What is a cut
scene](guide:scenes/what-is-a-cut-scene).
