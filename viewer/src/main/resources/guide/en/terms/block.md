---
title: Block
kind: term
summary: A square of 8 by 8 cells, 128 units wide, the piece in which a geodata file is written.
related: geodata/formats
---
A block is flat, with one height for all its cells, or keeps one floor in each cell, or several floors in some cells.
Flat blocks keep the files small. See [Geodata formats](guide:geodata/formats).
