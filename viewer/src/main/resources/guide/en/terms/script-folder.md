---
title: Script folder
kind: term
summary: The folder of an L2OFF server with its text files, such as npcpos.txt and npcdata.txt.
related: spawns/read-spawns, npcs/npc-files, areas/see-areas, terms/areadata
---
A server keeps its game data as text in its `script` folder: spawns, NPCs, items, skills and areas. **NPC Spawns**,
**Areas**, **NPCs**, **Map events** and **Export location** read it. You choose it once in **NPC Spawns** or in
**Areas**, which share it, or as **Server scripts folder** on the page of the client in Settings. Cruma keeps it for
the client you opened. See
[Open and read spawns](guide:spawns/read-spawns) and [See areas in the world](guide:areas/see-areas).
