---
title: Playhead
kind: term
summary: The moment of a scene you work on, a line across the tracks of the tray.
related: scenes/video-director, scenes/keys-and-shots
---
A press on the ruler moves the playhead, and the world shows that moment of the scene. A new key, actor or sound goes in
at the playhead, and Play starts there. See [The video director](guide:scenes/video-director).
