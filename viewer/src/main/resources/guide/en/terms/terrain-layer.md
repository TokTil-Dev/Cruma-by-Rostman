---
title: Terrain layer
kind: term
summary: One texture of the ground, such as grass or rock, with a mask that says where and how much it shows.
related: edit/paint-the-ground, terms/terrain
---
The mask is a grey picture over the whole tile, called the layer mask. The game draws the layers in order, and a later
layer covers an earlier one where its mask is bright. Painting changes the masks, not the textures. See [Paint the
ground](guide:edit/paint-the-ground).
