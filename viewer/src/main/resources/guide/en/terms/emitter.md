---
title: Emitter
kind: term
summary: A part of an effect that throws particles: flat sprites, small meshes, or a beam.
related: effects/what-are-effects, effects/edit-an-effect, edit/select-and-move
---
An effect class holds one emitter or more, and together they make what you see. Each emitter decides how many
[particles](guide:terms/particle) start, where, how fast they fly, and their colour and size. A row of the **Effects**
panel counts them, such as `5 sprites · 1 mesh`. See [What are effects](guide:effects/what-are-effects).

A map also places effects, such as the flame of a torch or the smoke over a chimney. In a map such an effect is an
[actor](guide:terms/actor) named like `Emitter36`, and Edit moves, turns, copies and deletes it by its mark, see
[Select and move objects](guide:edit/select-and-move).
