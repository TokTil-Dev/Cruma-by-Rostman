---
title: npcgrp.dat
kind: term
summary: The client's table of how each NPC looks: its mesh, textures, pawn class and size, by id.
related: npcs/what-is-npc-data, npcs/export-an-npc
---
`npcgrp.dat` lies in the client's `system` folder. The client draws an NPC with the record of its id, the same id as
in the server's [npcdata.txt](guide:terms/npcdata). C1 and C2 have a text table, `npcgrp.txt`, in its place. See
[What is NPC data](guide:npcs/what-is-npc-data).
