---
title: Static mesh
kind: term
summary: A shape made of triangles that does not bend, such as a tree, a rock or a lamp, placed in a map.
related: start/how-a-map-is-built
---
Static meshes are kept in `.usx` packages in `StaticMeshes`. One mesh can stand in many places, each one an
[actor](guide:terms/actor) with its own place, turn and size. See [How a map is built](guide:start/how-a-map-is-built).
