Look at effects and skills at once, without waiting for a world to load.

### You need

- A game client chosen on the start menu.

## Steps

1. Choose the client under **CLIENTS** at the left of the start menu.
2. Press **Effects** under **MORE**. The viewer opens at once with no map and the **Effects** panel open.
3. Choose an effect or a skill. It stands in the air ahead of the camera, and the camera comes to it from 210 units.
4. Open **Scene settings** to choose what stands behind it: **Backdrop** in Atmosphere has **Dark** and
   **Client's sky**.
5. Choose **Main menu** in the Viewer menu to go back to the start menu. **RECENT** there lists **Effects**, and a
   click on its row opens it again.

When it worked, the effect plays over a dark grey, on which bright effects stand out.

Without a world, the viewer keeps only what works without one. Scene settings, Performance, Effects and AI agent, when
it is on, are on the tool rail, and the tools of a world, such as **NPC Spawns** and **Geodata**, are not. On **Dark**, **Time of day** still
sets the light on the characters and the hours of effects that follow the clock. The camera stays the free one, so
<kbd>O</kbd> does not turn it to the top view.

### If it went wrong

- An effect can hardly be seen: choose **Dark**. Most effects add light, and a pale sky hides them.
- The card has no **Here** or **Ground**: every place is the same without a world, so it needs none. **Frame** brings
  the effect into view.
